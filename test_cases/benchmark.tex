\section{Discussion and Structural Insights}
An analysis of the benchmarking profiles yields two key architectural insights:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Heterogeneous Paradigm Shifts:} Moving from multicore CPU environments (\texttt{gpMC}) to even entry-level professional GPUs (\texttt{MCCB-gpu} on RTX 2000) yields over an order of magnitude speedup (e.g., $27\times$ speedup at the largest medium scale).
    \item \textbf{Anomalous Scaling Profile:} In the \texttt{MCCB-gpu} dataset on the RTX 2000/4500 architectures, scaling from $N=\num{15626}$ to $N=\num{32768}$ demonstrates an absolute reduction in execution time. This indicates a hardware architectural threshold where GPU thread occupancy optimizations and block-level memory coalescing align to hit peak efficiency.
\end{itemize}
